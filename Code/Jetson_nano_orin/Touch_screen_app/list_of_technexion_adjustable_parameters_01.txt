Here is the full list of image processing property attributes available in the VizionSDK (pyvizionsdk), based directly on the constants exposed within the library's internal structures.

These properties can be used with functions like VxGetISPImageProcessingRange(), VxGetISPImageProcessing(), and VxSetISPImageProcessing().
1. Image Quality & Enhancement Properties

These adjust the basic visual style, color balance, and clarity of the frame feed:

    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_BRIGHTNESS (Adjusts overall light intensity)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_CONTRAST (Adjusts the distinction between light and dark areas)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_SATURATION (Adjusts color intensity and vividness)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_SHARPNESS (Enhances edges and fine details)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_GAMMA (Controls luminance curve and mid-tones)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_DENOISE (Controls the sensor digital noise reduction filter strength)

2. Exposure & Light Control Properties

These dictate how the sensor physically collects light and updates its gain behaviors:

    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_EXPOSURE_MODE (Toggle between Manual and Automatic Exposure loops)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_EXPOSURE_TIME (Manual shutter speed length)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_EXPOSURE_MIN_TIME (Floor limit for Auto-Exposure calculations)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_EXPOSURE_MAX_TIME (Ceiling limit for Auto-Exposure calculations)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_EXPOSURE_GAIN (Manual sensor digital/analog amplification level)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_BACKLIGHT_COMPENSATION (Boosts exposure on shadowed objects in front of bright backgrounds)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_FLICK_MODE (Anti-flicker settings, typically matching 50Hz/60Hz AC power frequencies to stop banding)

3. White Balance & Color Correction

    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_WHITEBALANCE_MODE (Toggle between Manual and Automatic White Balance)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_WHITEBALANCE_TEMPERATURE (Manual Kelvin temperature control to balance warm/cool cast shifts)

4. Advanced Sensor Modes (HDR & Triggering)

    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_EHDR_MODE (Enhanced High Dynamic Range operational toggle)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_TRIGGER_MODE (Configures external hardware/software sync trigger parameters instead of continuous streaming)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_EHDR_EXPOSURE_MIN_NUMBER
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_EHDR_EXPOSURE_MAX_NUMBER
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_SPECIAL_EFFECT_MODE (Applies filters like Grayscale, Negative, Sepia, etc.)

5. PTZ (Pan-Tilt-Zoom) & Geometry Alterations

If your camera lens or sensor cropping configuration supports digital or physical movement controls, these attributes dictate frame layout:

    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_ZOOM (Digital scaling modification factor)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_PAN (Horizontal sub-window alignment offset)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_TILT (Vertical sub-window alignment offset)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_FLIP_MODE (Rotates/mirrors the frame stream array orientation vertically or horizontally)
    vz.VX_ISP_IMAGE_PROPERTIES.ISP_IMAGE_JPEG_QUALITY (Compression quality ratio parameter if pulling compressed snapshot arrays)
